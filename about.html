Founder's Message

Dr. Vivienne Kachollom Rwang

Founder & CEO, Vichi Resources Consult

Welcome to Vichi Resources Consult.

I believe that lasting change in education begins with investing in people. When teachers are supported to develop their practice, school leaders are equipped to lead with purpose, and young people are encouraged to recognise their potential, the benefits reach far beyond the classroom. They strengthen families, create opportunities and help communities flourish.

This belief has grown through my experience as a teacher, school leader, university educator, researcher and mentor. Across these roles, I have encountered people with remarkable commitment and ambition who need practical guidance, encouragement and opportunities to develop. I have also seen how much becomes possible when we listen carefully to people, understand their circumstances and work alongside them.

I founded Vichi Resources Consult to help meet that need. My aim was to create a consultancy that brings educational knowledge into everyday practice, supporting people to turn their ideas and aspirations into meaningful action. Whether we are helping establish a school, facilitating professional development, mentoring young people or supporting a community learning initiative, our work begins with understanding the people involved and what they hope to achieve.

Inclusion is central to this purpose. My research into education for internally displaced children in Nigeria has deepened my commitment to learners whose voices and needs are too often overlooked. It has reinforced my conviction that quality education must respond to people’s circumstances, recognise their strengths and create opportunities for meaningful participation. Every learner deserves to feel valued, to belong and to have the opportunity to progress.

At Vichi, we bring together research, professional experience and local knowledge to offer support that is relevant and practical. We value the expertise that educators, families and communities already hold. Through collaboration, we help build confidence, strengthen skills and develop approaches that people can sustain and adapt as their needs change.

My vision is for Vichi Resources Consult to be a trusted partner for those working to improve education: a place where ideas are nurtured, challenges are thoughtfully addressed and people are supported to learn and lead.

Thank you for taking the time to discover our work. I warmly invite you to explore our services and programmes, share your aspirations with us, and consider what we might achieve together.

Dr Vivienne Kachollom Rwang Founder & CEO Vichi Resources Consult
