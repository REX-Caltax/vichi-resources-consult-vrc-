Dr. Vivienne Kachollom Rwang

Founder & CEO | Education Management, Leadership & Teacher Development

Dr. Vivienne Kachollom Rwang is an education consultant, researcher and mentor with over thirty years of experience across early years, schools, higher education and community learning. She combines practical teaching and leadership expertise with a commitment to inclusive, high-quality education.

She holds a PhD in Education and an MSc in Education Leadership and Management from the University of Southampton, United Kingdom, alongside a Postgraduate Diploma in Education from the University of Abuja. In 2024, while working at the University of Southampton, she was awarded Fellowship of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA), a UK professional recognition of her expertise and contribution to teaching and supporting learning in higher education.

Her experience includes serving as a headteacher, Teaching Fellow at the University of Southampton and Consultant Lecturer at IFSA London. She has organised and facilitated Continuing Professional Development (CPD) programmes locally and internationally, supporting teachers, school leaders and education professionals to strengthen their practice. Her expertise also spans school establishment and improvement, curriculum and assessment design, educational leadership and mentoring.

Vivienne’s doctoral research examined basic education provision for internally displaced children in Nigeria. Her wider research and collaborative work address inclusion, student voice and educational inequality, informing her approach to supporting educators and communities.

Her achievements include winning Southampton’s doctoral research poster showcase in 2022 and receiving the Vice Chancellor Award, student awards for pastoral support and approachability in 2023. In 2025, she received a Certificate of Honour from the Gbong Gwom Jos, the traditional ruler of her ethnic community, in recognition of her service.

Through Vichi Resources Consult, she brings together research, professional experience and a belief in people’s capacity to grow, lead and create lasting educational change. This is driven by her passion for people and learning, and commitment to inclusion and transformation.
