People make the difference

Meet the people behind VRC.

A team with experience across education, leadership, inclusion and community development.

Dr. Vivienne Kachollom Rwang

Education Management & Leadership

Dr. Vivienne has taught for more than twenty-five years. She earned a Master's in Education Management and Leadership from the University of Southampton in 2016 and a postgraduate diploma in education from the University of Abuja in 2006.

She specialises in early-years teaching, teacher development, school setup, and the planning, delivery and assessment of courses. She is a certified member of the Nigerian Institute of Training and Development and is pursuing a doctoral degree at the University of Southampton.

Katherine (Kate) Pam

Management Consultant

Kate has a first degree in agricultural economics and is a Certified Management Consultant and member of the Nigerian Management Institute.

Her experience includes wealth and project management, alongside MBAs in marketing, global management and leadership. Her interests include community-led sustainable development and education in Nigeria.

Simi Valerie Rwang

Early Years Teacher

Simi is passionate about working with children and teaches in a British-curriculum classroom environment.

Her training includes classroom management, the Early Years Foundation Stage, Jolly Phonics, practical modern teaching methods and classroom displays. She values understanding each child's individual learning style.
