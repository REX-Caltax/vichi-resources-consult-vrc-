Who we are

Education consultancy with a human-centered approach

Vichi Resources Consults (VRC) is a professional consultancy dedicated to strengthening educational practice through modern, practical, and effective strategies. We work with teachers, administrators, children, and learning communities to create a framework for continuous improvement.

Our work is rooted in the belief that quality education depends on the right people, the right methods, and the right environment. We support schools and education stakeholders with tailored programmes that promote excellence, inclusion, and growth.

What we offer

OUR SERVICES

Vichi Resources Consult uses its extensive knowledge of the education sector to provide flexible educational services designed to support institutions and individuals in reaching their maximum potential. Our wide range of services include:

Continuing Professional Development (CPD)

Our “Hands Up for Excellence” courses provide teachers with access to the best training opportunities locally and abroad, helping them share practice and stay current with global trends in teaching and management.

School Resourcing

We offer professional guidance on the most suitable resources for schools, including curriculum development, planning, books, classroom requirements, and essential equipment needed for quality teaching and learning.

Setting Up New Schools

We provide consultancy for establishing reputable schools with strong planning and a full understanding of global best practices, quality standards, and effective budget management.

Family-Centred Learning Projects

We explore practical ways to empower families as centres of learning, especially in communities with limited access to conventional schools, helping reduce costs while supporting early-years education.

Staff Recruitment

We recruit highly qualified academic and administrative staff, set up staffing structures, allocate roles, advise on welfare packages, and help develop job descriptions, handbooks, policy documents, and appraisal systems.

Applications for Digital Technology in Education and Welfare

We help communities benefit from digital innovations in education and welfare, bringing web-based solutions to both the young and older members of society in ways that improve access and participation.

Community Educational Projects

We promote knowledge-sharing and educational development in communities, supporting displaced settlements and rural areas through practical, culturally relevant learning initiatives.

Mentoring Programmes

We offer career guidance and mentoring support in primary and secondary schools, helping children build self-management, leadership, patriotism, good character, and responsible citizenship.

Educational Research Facilitation and Support

We connect schools and institutions with research opportunities, funding, and expert partnerships that support innovation, academic growth, and the development of national educational programmes.

Funtertainment

Funtertainment is an annual youth and children’s event designed to create memorable celebrations while helping children develop skills, confidence, and productive thinking through fun, educational, and entertaining experiences.

Our team

Experienced professionals with a passion for education

Vivienne Kachollom Rwang

Education Management & Leadership

Vivienne Kachollom Rwang took up teaching over twenty-five years ago. She obtained a Masters Degree in Education Management and Leadership from University of Southampton, United Kingdom in 2016, a postgraduate diploma in education (PGDE) at the University of Abuja in 2006.

Married and with young children of her own, she specialises not only in early years teaching but also in the professional development of teachers, setting up reputable, excellent standard schools and in the planning, delivery and assessment of courses of study.

She is British trained and has travelled and studied extensively throughout her career, including taking part in summer courses on teaching and learning in the foundation stage, “Every Child Matters” and Modern Strategies for Administration. She is a certified member of the Nigerian Institute of Training and Development (NITAD). She is presently working on a Doctoral Degree at the University of Southampton, United Kingdom.

Adam Meyersieck

Special Educational Needs Specialist

Adam Meyerisiek has been a ‘career’ educator, from his first degree in social and elementary education at Central Michigan University, USA. After a year’s experience in the United States teaching pupils with emotional and behavioural difficulties (EBD), he moved to the United Kingdom, first as a primary school teacher and since 2008 as a specialist working with Special Educational Needs (SEN) pupils.

He completed studies and submissions for MA (London) at the Institute of Education in 2014. He has combined teaching at the Samuel Rhodes SEN School with lectures at London Metropolitan University, tutoring with student teachers at the Institute of Education and contributing to professional development programmes and special education services in communities across the United Kingdom and around the world.

Katherine (Kate) Pam

Management Consultant

Katherine (Kate) Pam has her first degree in agricultural economics. Working in financial services, she went on to become a Certified Management Consultant and a member of the Nigerian Management Institute.

She now has over seven years of experience in wealth and project management. In the United Kingdom she has added MBAs in marketing, global management and leadership, and has also engaged in a variety of community-serving activities including work as a teaching assistant in a special educational needs school.

Her current interests lie in mobilising the strengths of families and local communities for peaceful, sustainable development, and in securing the substantial advances in education on which Nigeria’s future depends.

John Baker

Community & Communication Specialist

John Baker studied life sciences at Cambridge University, England, and health care in London. His career took him in many directions – his major activities always primarily concerned with effective communication.

He moved to east London in 1999, became a housing association director in 2001, and thereafter has been continuously involved in community and public affairs. His proposal for a partnership between family-focused social work and housing (2008) was the first of its kind in the United Kingdom.

He is a member of the board of his local health network, with a special interest in community psychological health matters. John strongly encourages empowerment and practical creativity, both traditional and technological, within communities in London where he has his own workshop.

Simi Valerie Rwang

Early Years Teacher

Simi Valerie Rwang is a young member of the team who is very passionate about working with children. Having attended a British school herself, she puts that experience to use in her work as a teacher.

She has gained more experience working with children in a classroom environment under the British curriculum.
