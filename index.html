Who we are

Education consultancy with a human-centered approach

Vichi Resources Consults (VRC) is a professional consultancy dedicated to strengthening educational practice through modern, practical, and effective strategies. We work with teachers, administrators, children, and learning communities to create a framework for continuous improvement.

Our work is rooted in the belief that quality education depends on the right people, the right methods, and the right environment. We support schools and education stakeholders with tailored programmes that promote excellence, inclusion, and growth.

Our mission

Our purpose is to provide bespoke educational services by setting up reputable high standard schools, offering hands on professional development opportunities locally and internationally in order to keep school abreast with current trends which would translate into excellence in teaching and learning.

Vision

Our purpose is to help education personnel develop the required skills and expertise by sharing practical, modern strategies that ensure effectiveness in performance and high levels of quality service.

Values

We believe in empowerment, creativity, inclusion, professionalism, and sustainable community development. These values shape every training and consultation programme we deliver.
