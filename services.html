What we offer

Good ideas, made useful in the real world.

Flexible educational services for schools, educators, families and communities, shaped around the work in front of you.

01

Continuing Professional Development

Our “Hands Up for Excellence” courses provide teachers with access to local and international training, shared practice and current approaches to teaching and management.

02

School Resourcing

Guidance on curriculum development, planning, books, classroom requirements and equipment for quality teaching and learning.

03

Setting Up New Schools

Consultancy for establishing reputable schools, with planning around best practices, quality standards and budget management.

04

Family-Centred Learning Projects

Practical ways to empower families as centres of learning, particularly in communities with limited access to conventional schools.

05

Staff Recruitment

Support with recruitment, staffing structures, role allocation, welfare packages, job descriptions, handbooks and appraisal systems.

06

Digital Technology in Education & Welfare

Web-based solutions that help communities use digital innovation to improve access and participation in education and welfare.

07

Community Educational Projects

Practical, culturally relevant learning initiatives that support knowledge-sharing in displaced settlements and rural communities.

08

Mentoring Programmes

Career guidance and mentoring to help young people build self-management, leadership, good character and responsible citizenship.

09

Research Facilitation & Support

Connections to research opportunities, funding and expert partnerships that support innovation and educational development.

10

Funtertainment

An annual youth and children’s event combining celebration with opportunities to develop skills, confidence and productive thinking.
