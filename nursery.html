Our vision

A place where every child is known, valued and inspired to flourish.

We nurture confident, compassionate and curious children in a Christ-centred, culturally rich environment. We envision a nursery where faith shapes character, diversity is celebrated, families are supported, and children build strong foundations for lifelong learning and purposeful living.

Our mission

Early education rooted in care, purpose and high standards.

  • Provide early years education grounded in Christian values of love, kindness, integrity and respect.
  • Deliver a high-quality EYFS curriculum supporting every child’s academic, emotional and spiritual development.
  • Create a culturally responsive environment that reflects and celebrates Southampton’s diverse communities.
  • Partner closely with parents and carers to support children’s growth at nursery and at home.
  • Uphold high standards of safeguarding, inclusion and professional practice.
  • Foster resilience, confidence and empathy through purposeful play, storytelling, reflection and positive relationships.
